# Wearable quality audit

Updated 2026-07-30. This inventory is append-only with respect to encoded IDs:
legacy IDs stay decodable even when an item is removed from the selectable UI.
`PASS` means the option clears the automated body, animation, silhouette, and
socket-fit gates. `REBUILT` additionally means its geometry was materially
re-authored in the current reference/img2threejs pass. The final multi-angle
render review remains part of the ten-round visual sign-off.

## Critical changes completed

- `None` on tops and legwear now exposes the stylized body instead of a baked
  shirt or dark baked pants. Stock shoe soles are hidden; `None` footwear is a
  bare stylized foot and Sandals supply their own visible foot.
- A pelvis yoke closes the old waist gap, and Overalls/Kilt now span the pelvis
  instead of reading as disconnected tubes or a floating bib.
- Hair is a separate, fully colorable slot with 20 styles. Hair stays visible
  under open accessories and hides under full hats.
- Cape and Wings are Back items. Shell, Satchel, and Air Tanks are legacy-only;
  Shades is legacy-only in Face because glasses belong in Eyewear.
- Held props use per-item grip transforms and are checked against both hands,
  the head, and the full run/victory/failure pose sweep.

## Remaining sign-off

The automated gates are green, but code-level clearance is not a substitute for
art direction. The final ten rendered review rounds must inspect front, rear,
both profiles, running, jumping, failure, and victory poses at the actual
wardrobe framing. Any item that reads as an unfinished primitive at that scale
returns to its author script and reference manifest for another generated pass.
