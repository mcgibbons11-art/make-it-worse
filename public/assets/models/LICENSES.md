# MAKE IT WORSE asset credits

All models below were downloaded from Sketchfab on July 26, 2026 under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 license. They were modified in Blender 4.5 for browser delivery: cameras and lights were removed; transforms, scale, and ground origin were normalized; and oversized textures were reduced where needed. Selected large files were further optimized with glTF Transform using Meshopt geometry compression and WebP textures. Game physics uses separate authored colliders.
